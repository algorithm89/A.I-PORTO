## What this track will cover

Most "math for AI" resources either treat you like a math major or like a child. This track aims for the honest middle: the *minimum* math you need to read modern AI papers, debug a model, and understand why your loss is doing what it's doing — explained the way I'd explain it to a friend over coffee.

The planned arc:

- **Linear algebra you'll actually use** — vectors, matrices, dot products, and why neural networks are basically just matrix multiplications with attitude.
- **Calculus you'll actually use** — derivatives, gradients, the chain rule, and what backpropagation is really doing under the hood.
- **Probability you'll actually use** — distributions, expectation, Bayes' rule, and where it shows up in classification, language models, and uncertainty estimates.
- **The bridge to code** — every concept paired with a short NumPy snippet so the math stops being abstract.
